About Altask Labs

Building AI-powered tools that make website creation faster, simpler, and more accessible.

Who we are

A small software lab with a single focus.

Altask Labs is an independent software studio building tools for creating websites. We pair AI with a visual editor so that going from an idea to a live, professional site doesn't require writing code or fighting with complicated software.

It's for founders, freelancers, small teams, and makers who need a real website but don't have a designer or developer on call, the people who know exactly what they want to say and just want the most direct path to publishing it.

We're not trying to be everything to everyone. We're focused on one problem: making it genuinely easy to build a website you're proud of.

Independent

Bootstrapped and Independent

Altask Labs is fully self-funded, built with founder savings and revenue from freelance software development. That independence means we answer to the people using the product rather than outside investors, and we get to build carefully, at our own pace.

Our story

It started with frustration.

I'd tried just about every website builder out there. Each one either boxed me into rigid templates, buried the settings I actually needed under endless menus, or spat out bloated pages I couldn't really control.

I kept coming back to the same thought: this should be simpler. Describe what you want, shape it visually, publish it. Instead of settling for the tools that existed, I started building the one I wished I had.

Altask grew straight out of that frustration, a platform that uses AI to handle the heavy lifting, then hands you a clean visual canvas to make the result truly yours.

What we're building

One platform, from idea to published.

Altask turns a simple prompt into a real, editable website, then gives you full visual control to shape every detail. The vision is a single place where creating, designing, and shipping a site all happen without friction.

AI website generation Visual editing Responsive by default Collaboration One-click publishing
Our principles

What guides our decisions

Not generic company values, the ideas we actually use when deciding what to build.

Simplicity over complexity.

Every feature has to earn its place. If it makes the product harder to understand, it doesn't ship.

Fast iteration on feedback.

We'd rather ship something small today and improve it from real usage than polish in private for months.

Your site, your control.

AI gets you started, but you stay in charge. Nothing important is locked away or out of reach.

Sustainable over trendy.

We build for the long term, not for whatever happens to be loud this quarter.

Looking ahead

Getting better, release by release.

We're continuously improving Altask, listening to the people who use it, sharpening the AI, and expanding what the editor can do. The mission stays the same: make professional website creation accessible to anyone, whatever their budget or background.

There's a lot still to build, and we're only just getting started.
