01 / START

Start from a template, or from nothing at all

Open one of the four templates and edit it, or begin with an empty page and drop in the sections you need. Either way you are working on the real thing from the first click — there is no draft to unpick and no generated markup to clean up.

  • Four templates you can open, edit and publish
  • Fourteen section presets: nav, hero, features, pricing, FAQ, footer and more
  • Every section is an ordinary component the moment it lands
02 / EDIT

The drag-and-drop editor that doesn't fight you

Click any text on the canvas to edit it inline. Drag blocks to reorder. Use the inspector for properties. Snapping, alignment guides, and 80 levels of undo, it feels like a design tool, except what you're editing is the live website.

  • 15+ component types, fully styleable
  • Sites saved to your account, on any device
  • Keyboard-first: ⌘D, ⌘↑/↓, ⌘Z all work
03 / SHIP

Hit publish, your site is live in 1.2 seconds

From canvas to a live site with HTTPS, on your own domain. Static files on a CDN, one stylesheet, no runtime. No deploy scripts, no DNS forms, no certificate emails.

  • 220+ edge locations, median TTFB under 120ms
  • Plain HTML and one stylesheet, nothing to un-bundle later
  • Export the whole site as files at any time
